PYROTECHNICFX T190 RECEIVER v1.6.3
==================================
Relay 1 control output: GPIO1
Relay 2 control output: GPIO2
Relay 3 control output: GPIO3
Relay 4 control output: GPIO4
Relay 5 control output: GPIO15
Relay 6 control output: GPIO16

Default firmware is configured for active-low relay-driver inputs.
Do not connect relay coils directly to GPIO pins. Use suitable drivers, common
ground where required, flyback suppression for inductive loads, and a separate
relay power supply.
