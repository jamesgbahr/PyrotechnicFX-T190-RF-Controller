PYROTECHNICFX T190 TRANSMITTER v1.6.3
=====================================
Relay 1: onboard USER button, GPIO21
Relay 2: external normally-open momentary button, GPIO1 to GND
Relay 3: external normally-open momentary button, GPIO2 to GND
Relay 4: external normally-open momentary button, GPIO3 to GND
Relay 5: external normally-open momentary button, GPIO4 to GND
Relay 6: external normally-open momentary button, GPIO15 to GND

All inputs use INPUT_PULLUP. Pressed = connected to GND.
